# Common Frame

- Product / audience: A city independent-film screening club for people choosing their next shared viewing.
- Primary workflow: Browse a curated programme, open a screening, save a date, revisit saved screenings, and export a calendar entry.
- Primary object: A named film with an image, synopsis, screening time, duration, format, and discussion theme.
- Primary job: Choose a screening without losing the film's identity or programme context.
- Visible result: A saved screening appears in My programme and can be removed or exported as a calendar entry.
- Content readiness: Two real licensed films with official imagery and an explicitly labelled sample programme. No real booking, venue, member count, or availability claim.
- Language / locale: English; city is intentionally unspecified. Dates use a sample November 2026 programme.
- Constraints: Local static frontend; no external submission, payment, sign-up service, or backend. Semantic native controls, keyboard and touch access, reduced motion, recoverable media and storage failure.
- Non-goals / avoid: Ticket checkout, fake registration success, a fixed film-website template, copied brand assets, repetitive promotional bands.

Original one-sentence brief: 做一个城市独立电影放映俱乐部的网站.

The audience, language, brand, programme, and interactions above are implementation assumptions derived for this trial, not additional user input. Keep those assumptions visible in the trial evidence.
