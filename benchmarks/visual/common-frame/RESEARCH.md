## Material decision

The [Vertov still](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Man_with_a_movie_camera_1929_2.png) has a 487x400 original and is rejected for the intended reading distance. Generation was considered, but the built-in service rejected gpt-image-2 as unsupported by the configured account group. No image was returned and no fallback API was invoked. The implementation uses original official Tears of Steel and Sintel imagery with real film identities and source-specific credit. It uses a different official 4K frame for the first programme row and one official David Revoy storyboard as a process artifact, so the page does not repeat the lead image or make the production claim through copy alone. The Elephants Dream download is retained as rejected acquisition evidence because of its visible title watermark; it is never used in the page. Dates remain sample data. See assets/SOURCES.md.

The first full audit also exposed a background-priority defect: the concrete uncatalogued creation request inherited image from PRODUCT.md and selected creative-editor. Concrete creation requests now keep their current subject; contextual refinement still inherits product facts. CLI readback is adaptive-surface / current-request / open-experience for this trial.

The live 5Point Film Festival site was followed from its Siteinspire entry and inspected at 1440x900 and 390x844. Its hero combines a strong typographic statement with a separate moving image field, and its mobile version changes the navigation and preserves the statement/image tension rather than shrinking the desktop grid. Common Frame borrows the idea of unequal visual roles, but translates it into a quieter film programme: the second screening reverses the desktop image/text relationship, while mobile returns to a readable sequence. This is a relationship reference, not a copied skin.

## Three structural hypotheses

1. Film-poster wall: immediate scan, but no lead editorial point and too much equal visual weight. Keep as an alternative, not the chosen structure.
2. Immersive film + attached programme: one bounded opening shot with brand and modest caption, followed promptly by an open programme field. Chosen for its strong subject and direct visitor path.
3. Typographic calendar with intermittent stills: excellent schedule comparison, but the new club's culture remains weaker before visitors know the films. May replace the choice if the real render loses utility.
