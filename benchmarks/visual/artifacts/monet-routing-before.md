### Hard Invariants

- **Semantic accessibility**: Keep semantic structure, keyboard and touch access, visible focus, readable contrast, labels, and text scaling intact.
- **Task and state completeness**: When the surface has a task or workflow, keep its primary task, important non-default states, recovery path, and final state understandable and actionable. When it is authored, narrative, or exploratory, keep its chosen attention path, transitions, spatial relationships, and ending coherent instead.
- **Responsive usability**: Re-stage the experience for the target viewport and input model; do not preserve a beautiful desktop composition by breaking mobile use.
- **Asset truth and rights**: Use user-owned, attributable, authorized, generated, or deliberate assetless media; do not present placeholders, uncertain rights, or fabricated proof as real.
- **Motion safety and fallback**: Motion needs a purpose, cleanup, reduced-motion behavior, interruption handling, and a usable static or final-state fallback.
- **Performance and runtime integrity**: Keep the target runnable, bounded, and honest about loading, failure, dependency, and performance tradeoffs.
- **Rendered proof**: Do not claim visual quality from prose or a build alone; verify the relevant rendered states and label unverified claims.

## 20-Round Design Production Loop

- Status: **ready**
- Surface: **Experience** (medium)
- Design operation: **Animate** (medium)
- Rule: Twenty checkpoints, not twenty blind rewrites. Batch independent observations, but every round must end in a decision, artifact, rendered comparison, or explicit skipped-with-reason record.
- Divergence: Rounds 1-9 create and narrow choices; do not polish a weak direction before the verdict.
- Construction: Rounds 10-15 turn the verdict into a complete, runnable surface before detail polish.
- Verification: Rounds 16-20 use actual renders, fresh eyes, responsive/state evidence, and a final evidence pack.
- Stop: Stop only when the declared scope is complete and the last material defect has a repair or an explicit blocker. Do not count a green build as visual acceptance.

- 1. **context** · Read the request, project, audience, device, and existing visual authority. Output: `requirement-frame`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 2. **job** · Name the visitor's primary job and the result that proves it. Output: `surface-mode`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 3. **constraints** · Separate hard invariants from preferences, risks, and reversible choices. Output: `constraint-register`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 4. **evidence** · Inspect routes, components, tokens, content, assets, runtime entrypoints, and sibling surfaces. Output: `evidence-map`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 5. **references** · Choose references by missing job; extract visible relationships instead of copying skins. Output: `reference-ledger`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 6. **vernacular** · Find the subject's materials, artifacts, culture, language, and visual vocabulary. Output: `subject-thesis`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 7. **defaults** · Run a first-order and second-order AI-default scan before choosing a direction. Output: `anti-default-review`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 8. **diverge** · Create 3-5 structurally different directions, not cosmetic palette variants. Output: `concept-set`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 9. **converge** · Choose one direction against subject fit, hierarchy, emotional register, feasibility, and responsive viability. Output: `direction-verdict`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 10. **contract** · Write the chosen composition, type, geometry, material, motion, asset, and exception contract. Output: `design-contract`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 11. **system** · Extract tokens, type roles, component families, icon grammar, and page-specific overrides. Output: `design-system`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 12. **storyboard** · Plan the full surface, states, route transitions, responsive continuation, and ending. Output: `surface-storyboard`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 13. **assets** · Source or generate production-quality media and reject placeholders, fake metrics, and weak crops. Output: `asset-inventory`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 14. **skeleton** · Build the semantic and interactive skeleton with real content and the intended container model. Output: `working-surface`; evidence: decision or saved design artifact.
- 15. **baseline** · Run the target and capture the actual desktop, mobile, and relevant state before judging polish. Output: `baseline-captures`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.
- 16. **macro** · Fix the biggest visible defect first: scope, silhouette, hierarchy, composition, content, or media. Output: `macro-revision`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.
- 17. **responsive** · Re-stage the design for mobile, tablet, touch, text wrapping, and input behavior. Output: `responsive-captures`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.
- 18. **states** · Exercise loading, empty, error, success, focus, reduced-motion, transition, and recovery paths. Output: `state-captures`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.
- 19. **fresh-eyes** · Compare before/after and run an unprimed critique without implementation history bias. Output: `independent-critique`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.
- 20. **signoff** · Run static, runtime, visual, accessibility, and scope checks; record what remains open and update design memory. Output: `evidence-pack`; evidence: rendered capture, comparison, or verified check.

- include the relevant loading, empty, error, fallback, reduced-motion, or stable-ending path
- exercise the complete first meaningful path and capture rendered evidence before handoff
- the primary task can be completed from entry to visible result, with recovery when it fails
- all important product states named by the brief or component grammar are represented and reachable
- primary actions have a real destination, handler, or deliberate disabled reason; no dead CTA remains

Reject as incomplete:
- a first viewport presented as the finished page when the brief implies more content
- lower sections represented by headings, empty rectangles, repeated filler cards, or TODO copy
- a CTA, nav item, specimen control, or chapter marker that has no destination or state
- desktop-only completion with mobile overflow, missing media, or a collapsed reading order
- motion or imagery used to conceal unfinished content, missing states, or missing fallback

Proof required:
- desktop screenshot of the complete intended scope
- target-mobile screenshot of the complete intended scope
- inspected baseline and recapture with observed defects, implemented repairs, and regression checks
- selected image revision and translated code/asset changes when generation was used; generated images are not runtime evidence
- one exercised end-to-end path, non-default state, transition, or stable ending
- console/build output and asset provenance where applicable
