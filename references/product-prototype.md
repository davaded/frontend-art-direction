# Product Or Experience Signal Gate

Use this gate when a result feels generic, unfinished, or disconnected from its own subject. First choose the surface's mode. Functional products need a usable vertical slice. Narrative, art-directed, and experimental work needs a visible thesis, subject, attention path, and authored proof. The gate protects intent without limiting the type of design.

## Product Signal Contract

Before visual polish, write the contract that belongs to the work:

```text
Product object:
User and job:
Core loop:
Primary action:
Visible result:
Concrete content/data:
State matrix:
First viewport proof:
Prototype interaction:
```

For a functional product, use the fields above. For an authored experience, use:

```text
Mode: narrative / art-directed / experimental
Subject or world:
Audience intent:
Attention path:
Creative thesis:
Proof:
Transition or ending:
```

The subject may be a record, document, image, hardware object, project, chapter, media item, component specimen, place, person, atmosphere, abstract idea, or another real or deliberately authored material. A strong direction may choose its own object, medium, interaction, or lack of interaction.

## Functional Draft Floor

A functional product draft passes the floor when the first viewport contains:

1. One real object, artifact, record, or media item with specific content.
2. One primary action attached to that object.
3. One realistic state transition with visible feedback.
4. One meaningful loading, empty, error, partial, selected, or recovery state.
5. The next proof, result, or continuation entering the viewport.

Build this vertical slice before filling lower sections or adding ambient effects. The first interaction can be small: select a record, change a finish, open a project, adjust an image, run a command, switch a chapter, or toggle playback. It must change product state and explain what happened.

## Authored Experience Floor

An authored, narrative, or experimental surface passes its floor when:

1. The subject, world, or material is specific enough to carry the composition.
2. The point of view and formal thesis are visible without an explanatory essay.
3. The attention path has a deliberate order, tension, rhythm, or spatial relationship.
4. Real media, text, artifact, sound, object, or authored content supplies proof.
5. One reveal, transition, spatial relationship, or stable ending completes the chosen experience.

This floor does not require a CTA, database, dashboard, state matrix, card system, or conventional interaction. It can be satisfied by a poster-like composition, an editorial sequence, an atmospheric scene, a physical object study, or a deliberately static work when that is the chosen form.

## Translation By Intent

- App or tool: selected work item plus an action and its updated state.
- Hardware or brand story: actual object plus inspect/configure/compare behavior and concrete proof.
- Editorial or portfolio: actual artifact plus open/next/filter behavior and context.
- Media: actual item plus playback or inspection state.
- Component gallery: a working specimen with a meaningful state, not a screenshot wall.
- Narrative / art direction: choose the subject, point of view, formal device, and attention path; do not translate it into a product workflow unless the brief asks for one.
- Unknown category: form an independent thesis first. Do not infer commerce, dashboard, portfolio, or any other page genre merely because the request says “website”.

## Shell Rejection

Reject a functional surface when its main evidence is a slogan, oversized heading, CTA, decorative image, gradient, generic feature grid, or fake metrics while the object, workflow, result, and state feedback remain absent. Reject an authored surface only when it has no subject, thesis, attention path, or proof. Change the composition and content decision first; color, shadows, and motion cannot repair missing intent.

References may influence material, rhythm, interaction cadence, sequence, and visual proof after the work's own signal is chosen. They may replace local visual vocabulary when the user, project, inspected source, accepted concept, or strong model proposal owns the direction, but they never replace the target's subject or authorship.

## Proof

Capture the first viewport at desktop and the target mobile width. For products, exercise the primary action and record the before/after state. For authored work, record the chosen transition, spatial relationship, or stable ending. A successful build, a polished static hero, or a brief alone does not pass this gate.
