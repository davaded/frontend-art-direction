# Premium Finish

“高级”和“有质感”不是黑色、留白、渐变、玻璃或大标题的同义词。它来自一组互相支持的决定：真实对象、光学层级、稳定比例、可信材质、精确文案、完整状态和克制的细节。

## The Finish Contract

Every substantial surface must answer these questions before polish:

```text
Primary object:
Primary action:
One authored visual idea:
Type hierarchy and ceiling:
Surface/material logic:
Spacing rhythm and alignment axis:
Asset provenance and crop:
State feedback and motion purpose:
What was deliberately removed:
```

If the answer is “the gradient / the animation / the rounded cards,” the direction is not finished.

## Six Signals Of Quality

### 1. Object truth

The first screen shows the actual product, work object, data relationship, or authored media that earns the claim. A decorative background cannot carry product meaning.

### 2. Optical hierarchy

Typography, spacing, alignment, and contrast are tuned by eye after the mechanical grid is in place. Use one primary type family unless a second family has a real editorial job. Keep display type earned, not merely large.

### 3. Material discipline

Use a small surface system: canvas, object surface, elevated/active surface, divider, and semantic states. Depth should come from tonal contrast, edge treatment, shadow behavior, and material-specific detail. Do not stack glass, blur, glow, noise, and gradients to manufacture depth.

Use no more than two primary corner radii and three elevation treatments unless the product object requires more. Keep borders, shadows, and translucency optically consistent; a button, card, dialog, and hero should not each invent a different material language.

### 4. Composition with tension

Give the page one dominant object and one supporting counterweight. Use asymmetry only when the reading path remains clear. Avoid equal cards, repeated split sections, centered-everything heroes, and empty space with no object or copy to justify it.

### 5. Specific language

Replace generic claims with concrete product facts, verbs, dimensions, materials, constraints, and outcomes. Short copy with a real subject feels more expensive than a large paragraph with no evidence.

### 6. Quiet interaction

Hover, focus, press, selection, loading, and route continuity should feel tactile and immediate. Use one recognizable interaction signature, then stop. The final state must be clear without motion and the interaction must work on keyboard and touch.

Prefer stable responsive type steps over viewport-scaled headline tricks. Do not use negative letter spacing to force a “luxury” wordmark or heading to fit. Tune line length, line-height, and optical weight with the actual language and content.

## Red Flags

Downgrade and recompose when the first pass relies on:

- oversized display type carrying a weak concept;
- a full-page gradient, glow, blur, noise, or glass layer;
- identical rounded cards for hero, sections, metrics, and controls;
- black-and-gold or beige “luxury” styling without material evidence;
- stock atmosphere, fake product renders, or generic AI copy;
- excessive empty space hiding missing content;
- tiny gray text, weak contrast, or borders that disappear in real states;
- micro-interactions added before hierarchy, content, and object identity are correct.

## Finish Pass Order

1. Remove one unnecessary visual treatment.
2. Fix the first-read hierarchy in grayscale.
3. Tune type size, weight, line-height, and line length with real copy.
4. Tune spacing and alignment at the actual viewport instead of by isolated component.
5. Tune surfaces, borders, shadows, and material detail.
6. Add one purposeful interaction and verify its quiet fallback.
7. Compare desktop and mobile screenshots, then fix the largest visible mismatch.

Premium finish is accepted only when the result still feels intentional with the accent color, animation, and decorative effects removed.
