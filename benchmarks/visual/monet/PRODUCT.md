# In The Light

An independently authored visual essay about three actual Claude Monet paintings in the Art Institute of Chicago collection. It serves a reader who wants to look closely, connect an observation to the painting, and open the primary collection record. It is not a museum website, exhibition, ticket service, or artist commission.

The three paintings are Cliff Walk at Pourville (1882), Stacks of Wheat (End of Summer) (1890-91), and Water Lilies (1906). Dates, materials, dimensions, and collection credits come from the saved official API response. Looking notes are the author's interpretations, not quotations from Monet or the museum.

Complete scope: photographic opening, authored introduction, wheat painting/observation, water painting/observation, an ending that returns to the works, source records, native whole-painting/detail viewer with previous/next and dismissal, keyboard focus continuity, desktop/mobile restaging, reduced motion, actual asset loading and image-error recovery. No account, purchase, bookmark, or invented visitor metric is needed for this essay.
