# PRODUCT.md

- Product / audience: Common Ground, a photographic architecture notebook for readers observing buildings, materials, and light
- Primary job: Read three architectural observations and inspect the actual photographs
- Primary workflow: View the opening photograph -> browse observations -> open the full-resolution viewer -> read the field notes and credits
- Primary object: Three credited architectural photographs
- Content readiness: Full-resolution photographs and authored observational text; locations and authors come from the photo sources
- Hard constraints: Keep the photographers' attribution; do not invent commissioned projects or business claims
- Non-goals / avoid: Ecommerce, fabricated clients, decorative 3D, a generic feature grid
