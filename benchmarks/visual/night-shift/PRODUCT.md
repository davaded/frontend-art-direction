# Night Shift

Night Shift is an independent field guide for one observed object: the Whirlpool Galaxy (M51). It helps a visitor move from a wide astronomical image to a closer reading of the galaxy's spiral structure, then leave with a source-backed note. It is a reading and inspection experience, not a telescope marketplace, science dashboard, or museum reproduction.

The complete surface includes the opening image, an authored orientation, a selectable image reading distance, a compact observing note, source records, keyboard-operable controls, responsive continuation, reduced motion, and an image failure message.
