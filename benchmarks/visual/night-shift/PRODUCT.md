# Night Shift

Night Shift is an independent field guide for one observed encounter: the Whirlpool Galaxy (M51) and its companion. It moves between two complementary records of that encounter, from a wide astronomical field to a Hubble close view of the spiral structure, then leaves the reader with a source-backed note. It is a reading and inspection experience, not a telescope marketplace, science dashboard, or museum reproduction.

The complete surface includes the opening image, an authored orientation, a selectable image reading distance, a compact observing note, source records, keyboard-operable controls, responsive continuation, reduced motion, and an image failure message.
